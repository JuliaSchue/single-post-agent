# Step 4: Audience + niche

**Goal:** two short files that tell the agent who the posts are for and what the scan looks for.

## What you do

Interview, one question at a time. Use the user's own words.

`brand/audience.md`: who they help in one line, what those people sell or do, 5 sentences their clients say (mark each one as "heard from clients" or "my wording", and never present your own wording as a quote), what they already tried, what they want.

`brand/niche.md`: the niche in one line and the person's angle, 3 or 4 content pillars, topics that are off-limits, 3 to 5 public Instagram accounts in the niche to watch (never to copy), 5 to 10 hashtags.

## Done when

- both files exist, `niche.md` has at least 3 accounts and 5 hashtags, you checked that the accounts are public and posted recently, and the user approved both in words

**STOP:** tick, ask about step 5.
